Utah Nuclear Energy

The laws, permits, boards and sites behind Utah's nuclear buildout, from the public record. We report public records. You decide.

Posted Oct. 29, 2026 · Updated Oct. 29, 2026

The short version

  • Since 2023, Utah has passed laws creating a nuclear advisory board, a state nuclear regulator, an energy fund and a nuclear campus law.
  • A test reactor reached criticality at a state-owned lab in Emery County in June 2026 under a federal Department of Energy approval, not an NRC license.
  • The state trust land board approved a lease agreement with the same company for 640 acres in Carbon County; the terms list data centers among its uses.
  • Utah is a finalist for a federal nuclear campus, with Tooele County as the bid site. A report on state laws "that would inhibit the state's ability to host a campus" was due Oct. 31.
WHERE
Statewide · Emery, Carbon, Tooele, Box Elder, Weber and San Juan counties · Camp Williams · Hill AFB
