Carbon County: the Wellington Lease

The state trust land board voted unanimously to approve a lease agreement with Valar Atomics for 640 acres north of Wellington. Every public speaker who addressed it was in favor. Here are the terms.

Posted Oct. 29, 2026 · Updated Oct. 29, 2026
Read the plain-words version →

The short version

  • On Sept. 17, 2026, the Trust Lands board voted, as announced unanimously, to approve a 50-year lease agreement for 640 acres north of Wellington with Valar Atomics (480 acres now, 160 more if the National Guard moves), with a $400,000 signing bonus and rent starting at $30 an acre.
  • The lease lists "partnering with data centers" among its uses. It bars spent-fuel storage on trust land and says the water there is "fully appropriated."
  • Every public speaker who addressed the lease at both board meetings was in favor, including Carbon County's three commissioners and two deputy directors of the state energy office that is Valar's landlord at the San Rafael lab.
  • Valar has also asked the federal government for about 9,000 acres near Price. The number of reactors reported ranges from about 70 to 456; neither is in a federal record we found.

Where it is

General areas only. The lease is in Section 16, Township 14 South, Range 11 East; the federal request is reported as "near Price."

The Trust Lands vote

Aug. 20, 2026: the Trust Lands Administration board discussed the lease. No motion or vote. Speakers: Carbon County Commissioners Larry Jensen, Tony Martines and Jared Haddock; Jake Garfield, deputy director of the Office of Energy Development, whose office is Valar's landlord at the San Rafael lab; and Jess Housekeeper of Valar. All who addressed the lease spoke in favor (one online speaker, Sean Jordan, did not address Valar). The agency's Advocacy Office director, Liz Mumford, "expressed support." The minutes list trustees John Baza and Mike Mower as absent but also record both asking questions. We report both until the audio settles it.

Sept. 17, 2026: approved. Trustee Mike Mower moved to approve "on the terms set forth in the board memorandum." Chair Mike Nelson announced the vote as unanimous; the transcript does not make clear who seconded, and records no individual votes. Everyone who spoke about the lease was in favor, including Valar's Harry Hansen, Jess Housekeeper, Terry Bell and Ben Orgill, and Cory Gardner (described by East Idaho News as Valar's head of commercial development); Commissioners Jensen, Haddock and Martines (online: "They have nothing to hide"), and Office of Energy Development deputy director Tracy Rees: "I just cannot recommend strongly enough a partnership with Valor" (spelling as in the transcript).

Chair Nelson said: "We weren't always sure what to ask about... this was a new project for us at the trust." Staff said there is no lockout period. Kael Weston, a Federalism Commission member, spoke online about workforce housing, not the lease. The motion approved the agreement on the memo's terms and had staff finalize it; the signed lease was not found posted.

Federal land and the county

Valar has filed an initial application with the federal Bureau of Land Management for about 9,000 acres near Price ("Project Beehive"). BLM says it is "currently reviewing the application for completeness." If it moves forward, a notice of realty action, an environmental review and public comment would follow. No serial number or filing date was found in a federal record.

How many reactors? NPR reported company documents describing "some 456" reactors and about 9.6 gigawatts. KUTV reported "about 70." Neither number appears in any federal record we found, so we do not treat either as fact.

Oct. 6, 2026, Carbon County Planning Commission: Valar presented; CEO Isaiah Taylor said, "If the community doesn't want us here, we won't do it" (KUTV). No public comment was taken and no action was taken. The same night, a separate gas-powered "Technology Park" permit (Altigen, 1,236 acres east of Wellington) was tabled to Nov. 10.

County Commission agendas for June 17, Sept. 16 and Oct. 7, 2026 showed no Valar, data center or nuclear item. A county statement reported July 3, 2026 said some companies would not explore the county without nondisclosure agreements. Whether the county holds one with Valar was not found.

Questions to ask, and to whom

  • Ask: Trust Lands Administration — Where is the signed lease, and what appraisal supports the rent and purchase prices?
  • Ask: Carbon County Commission — Does the county have a nondisclosure agreement with Valar or any data center company? When will residents get a public hearing on the project?
  • Ask: Bureau of Land Management, Price Field Office — What is Project Beehive's serial number, and when will public comment open?
