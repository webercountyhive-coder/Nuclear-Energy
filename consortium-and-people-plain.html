The Nuclear Consortium and the People

An advisory board created in 2025, a closed session on real property on its agenda, a report that was due Oct. 31, and the names that show up on more than one state energy board.

Posted Oct. 29, 2026 · Updated Oct. 29, 2026
Read the full version with every source →

The short version

  • Utah's Nuclear Energy Consortium advises the state and the Legislature on nuclear development. The Office of Energy Development director, Emy Lesofski, chairs it and also chairs three other state energy boards.
  • Its Mar. 25, 2026 agenda listed a closed session on buying, exchanging or leasing real property. No minutes or outcome were found.
  • The law ends the consortium July 1, 2027. The interim committee chair, Sen. Derrin Owens, said he "has opened a bill file to repeal the sunset date."
  • Its member list changed between April and October 2026: Jamie Barnes was added and Joel Ferry no longer appears. The page doesn't say why.
WHERE
Statewide · Office of Energy Development · meetings at the Capitol and the San Rafael lab (Emery County)

  • Jerry Fenn, Laura Nelson (INL), Jamie Barnes — members
  • Sen. Derrin Owens — interim committee chair; said he opened a bill file to repeal the July 1, 2027 end date

In plain words

The Nuclear Energy Consortium is a small state advisory group created in 2025. It gives advice on nuclear power to a state energy office and to lawmakers. The head of that energy office runs it.

We found one meeting agenda online, for Mar. 25, 2026. It listed a private session about buying, trading or leasing property. We found no notes from any meeting.

A report from this group and the energy office was due Oct. 31, 2026. It is supposed to suggest changes to state law to make room for a "nuclear innovation zone," including nuclear power next to data centers. We had not found it as of Oct. 29.

The law says the group ends in July 2027. The senator who chairs the energy committee said he started a bill to remove that end date.

Questions to ask, and to whom

  • Ask: Office of Energy Development (Tracy Rees, consortium contact) — Which real property was discussed in the Mar. 25, 2026 closed session, and when will minutes be posted?
  • Ask: Office of Energy Development — Where is the S.B. 135 barrier analysis and nuclear innovation zone report that was due Oct. 31, 2026?
  • Ask: Sen. Derrin Owens — Will the bill to repeal the consortium's end date also require posted minutes and public comment at each meeting?
